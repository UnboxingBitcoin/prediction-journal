How it works

  1. Each day, write one prediction with a confidence and a date you'll know by.

  3. After 20–30 scored, Stats shows where your confidence runs hot or cold.

Your journal lives on this device and, once you log in with Nostr, encrypted on your relays. It does not depend on Claude.
